About ProofLoom

ProofLoom is a retrieval-augmented answer engine where provenance is first-class. Documents are split with LangChain, retrieval runs over a per-account index, and a LangGraph pipeline grades the evidence and composes a cited answer — generatively when a zero-cost model is configured, extractively otherwise. Every run is then anchored as a block on a hash-chained, proof-of-work ledger that anyone on the account can verify with one click.

Four threads, one fabric: RAG, LangChain, LangGraph, Blockchain.

Privacy policy

ProofLoom stores, per account: your email and name (optional), the documents you add, the questions you ask, the answers produced, and the ledger blocks that anchor them.

Documents and runs are scoped to your account and never shared with other accounts. Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2 (260,000 iterations). Sessions are signed tokens valid 30 days; signing out or deleting your account invalidates them.

Deleting your account removes your documents, runs and access immediately. Ledger blocks are append-only by design; a deletion is recorded as a new block, and no personal text remains in anchor payloads.

No analytics, no trackers, no third-party sharing. If an AI model key is configured server-side, your question and retrieved chunks are sent to that model provider to compose the answer; in extractive mode nothing leaves the service.

Questions about your data: shlok.is-a.dev.
